      Welcome

      Set up this computer for EAI

      Go from a new computer to your first EAI app with one guided setup. Enterprise AI Setup prepares the essentials, connects your account, and gets you ready to build.

      • Ready to build: the tools your EAI project needs are prepared for you
      • Secure access: sign in through your browser and keep credentials out of the installer
      • One guided flow: create your project and continue in your AI or editor workflow
      • No guesswork: your computer is checked first and only missing components are added

      Sign in to EAI

      Your browser handles sign-in. The installer never asks for or stores your password.

      What happens next

      Your browser opens for EAI authentication. When it finishes, setup continues automatically.

      New to Enterprise AI? Create your account on the website, then return here and choose Sign in with browser.

      Start your EAI app

      Choose a kebab-case project name and the folder where the app should be created or initialised.

      The supported eai init workflow fetches the Gofer assets and app template.

      OK

      EAI is ready to use

      Your app setup is complete. Continue in your chosen AI or editor host.

      eai whoami
      Gofer and the app template are installed by the supported eai init workflow. Enterprise AI Setup never stores your password or API secrets.
